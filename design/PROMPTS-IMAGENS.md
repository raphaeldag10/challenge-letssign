# Prompts das pranchas

Geradas pela ferramenta integrada de imagens, em modo de mockup. Referência visual: imagem institucional da LetsSign fornecida nesta conversa. Os prompts são instruções de geração visual, não código do aplicativo.

## Direção compartilhada

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished high-fidelity UI design presentation image, NOT code, for a native Android document signature app LetsSign, all text in Brazilian Portuguese. Use the attached institutional image ONLY as brand style reference: preserve the recognizable LetsSign white wordmark with the angled check mark on the right, purple/indigo palette and small cyan accents; do not reproduce the marketing photo or its promotional/legal claims.
Landscape high resolution board, preferably 3840x2160. FOUR equally sized full-height portrait Android screen artboards side by side in one horizontal row, each with a compact caption above. Screens must be large and legible. Flat straight-on Figma-like screens, subtle 12px rounded corners, NO perspective, NO device hardware, NO hands. Consistent 412x892dp proportions, status bar 9:41 and bottom Android gesture bar. Outside canvas light lavender #EEEAF6 with a restrained board heading top left and plenty of spacing. Screen background #F7F8FC, white paper/forms/list rows. Primary purple #6230B8, deep indigo #241475, cyan accent #20B7DD, dark body #202135, secondary #626879, separators #E2E4EC. Green #16734A signed; amber #956000 pending. Roboto font native Android, headings 24-28sp semibold, body 16sp, labels 14sp, minimum visual tap sizes 48dp. Left aligned content. Buttons solid purple with white text, 12dp corners. Inputs outlined, 12dp corners, clear labels above. Avoid every section boxed in cards, avoid decorative dashboards and tiny text. Use dividers and document rows. Distinctive brand gradient ONLY in login hero or compact top banner. Material-like outlined icons, functional hierarchy. DO NOT invent new features like payments, biometric login, legal certification or cloud providers.
Shared fictional case throughout: document "Contrato de prestação de serviços", file "contrato_servicos.pdf", 3 pages, 1,2 MB. Signers: Ana Lima (contratante, violet), Bruno Costa (contratado, blue), Carla Souza (testemunha, amber). Labels always distinguish individual and whole-document status. PDF legal body may be pale short typographic lines, never dense illegible nonsense. Exact important texts as quoted below. Show ALL four artboards fully, no truncation.
